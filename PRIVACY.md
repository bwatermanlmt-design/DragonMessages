# Privacy Policy for Dragon Messages

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Dragon Messages is an SMS and MMS application for Android. This policy describes what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

## Information the app collects

None. Dragon Messages has no account, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers. There is nothing to sign up for and nowhere your data goes.

## Messages

Your messages stay on your phone. Dragon Messages reads, sends, and files messages using Android's on-device message store, under the SMS permissions you grant when you set it as your default SMS app. Messages are never uploaded to any server. The published app has no network access at all.

## Contacts

The app reads your address book on your device so it can show contact names and file messages from people you know. Contact information is used only on your device. It is never uploaded, shared, or sold.

## Picture messages

For image-only messages, the app can read text contained in the image so your filing rules can act on it the same as on written text. This recognition runs entirely on your device. Images and recognized text never leave your phone. If recognition fails, filing proceeds without it.

## Permissions

The app requests only what it needs to be a texting app:

- SMS (send, receive, read) — its core purpose as your default SMS app.
- Contacts — to show names and file messages from people you know.
- Notifications — to alert you to new messages, including content-free alerts for private threads.
- Biometric — optional, only if you protect private threads with your device's biometric lock. Biometric data is handled by your device's operating system and never leaves your phone.

The published app requests no network permission and cannot access the internet.

## Third parties

None. The app sends no data to any third party, because it sends no data anywhere.

## Tipping

Settings includes an optional link to tip the developer via PayPal. Tapping it opens PayPal's website in your browser; the app sends no personal data to PayPal. Once you are on PayPal's site, that payment is governed by PayPal's own privacy policy and terms.

## Children

Dragon Messages is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone, child or adult.
